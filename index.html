The Thesis

Across quick-commerce infrastructure, brand-direct logistics, and indigenous defense manufacturing, the same pattern keeps showing up: a small, specialist fund gets there first — Shastra VC and Antler India at Inamo's seed, India Quotient at Blitz's — and by the time a brand-name fund shows interest, they're arriving as follow-on capital, not the lead. This is research built to find that window before it closes.

Method

Every number here is tagged, not just stated.

  • Confidence-rated sourcing

    Every figure marked HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW, not presented with false uniform authority.

  • Deal mechanics and return math

    Bear/base/bull scenarios with stated assumptions, not just a market-sizing slide.

  • A dissent case in every note

    The strongest argument against investing, written by the same person arguing for it.

  • Gaps are named, not hidden

    When a valuation or a founder's track record couldn't be verified, the note says so directly.
